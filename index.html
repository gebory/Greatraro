Why Raro Exists

Modern fashion moves quickly. Ideas disappear before they are understood. RARO exists as an archive where meaning comes before aesthetics, and where every mark exists for a reason.

The Mark

Continuation, not recreation

The RARO Mark draws inspiration from the ancient Mesopotamian rosette — a symbol found throughout the civilizations of Mesopotamia as a sign of continuity, identity and cultural memory.

Rather than reproducing the historical form directly, the mark was refined into a contemporary symbol.

Not a recreation. A continuation.
The symbol represents the archive itself.
